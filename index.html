About the Doctor

Theoretical Depth.
Practical Mastery.

Dr. Praveen Ayethepalle completed his Bachelor of Dental Science in India in 2007, before moving to Australia and furthering his education with a Master's degree in Forensic Odontology from the University of Western Australia in 2011 — giving him a uniquely analytical and science-driven approach to dental care.

He is well versed in all aspects of modern dental care, ranging from routine prevention right through to complex cosmetic surgery. His drive to practice dentistry stems from a genuine desire to help others manage their oral health and avoid suffering — especially for emergency patients in acute pain.

Dr. Ayethepalle is currently undertaking a two-year residency course in Orthodontics and Children's Dentistry, demonstrating his relentless commitment to expanding his clinical skills for the benefit of patients of all ages.
